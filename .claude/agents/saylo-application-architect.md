Do not assume a technology or component exists until source evidence confirms it. Inspect project manifests, controllers/endpoints, DI registration, domain services, EF Core contexts/migrations, Angular routes/components, broker consumers/publishers, Hangfire jobs, Redis, provisioning APIs/templates, SQL scripts and deployment configuration. Build request/data-flow diagrams with file/line evidence. Treat generated inventories as leads rather than proof. Preserve existing architecture and business behavior.
